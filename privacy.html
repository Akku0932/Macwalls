Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

🔒 No User Accounts

Download and run instantly. We never require passwords, names, emails, or phone numbers to use the app.

🛡️ No Personal Tracking

We do not monitor your screen, record keystrokes, track browsing, or inspect files on your Mac.

🚫 No Data Selling

We do not partner with data brokers, ad networks, or third-party marketing trackers. Period.

01. Our Privacy Principle

At Macwalls, we believe your Mac desktop is personal and private. Our software is designed to set aesthetic live wallpapers efficiently while respecting your privacy. Macwalls functions independently without requiring any login, registration, or cloud identity.

02. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in strict data minimization. Macwalls will NEVER collect, read, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, home address, or telephone number.
  • Your personal files, documents, photos, or desktop items.
  • Local video or image files you import into your personal playlist.
  • Screen recordings, window contents, or desktop screenshots.
  • Keystrokes, search history outside the app, or app usage outside Macwalls.
  • Payment card or financial credentials.

03. Anonymous Telemetry & Update Checks

To deliver automated software updates and monitor general application stability across macOS releases, Macwalls collects strictly non-personally identifiable, anonymous operational telemetry:

  • Anonymous Device Identifier: A randomly generated UUID stored in macOS UserDefaults to distinguish active installations from repeat launches. It is not tied to your Apple ID, real identity, or Mac serial number.
  • Application Version: Version string (e.g. 1.0.8) and build number to verify update eligibility via Sparkle.
  • System Metadata: macOS operating system version (e.g. macOS 15.1), hardware architecture (Apple Silicon vs Intel), and general device model string (e.g. MacBookPro18,1).
  • Display Count: Number of attached screens to optimize multi-display wallpaper rendering.

This information is used strictly to ensure update delivery, prevent application crashes, and track aggregate active user statistics.

04. Cryptographic Update Integrity (Sparkle)

Macwalls incorporates the open-source Sparkle 2 framework for silent, secure application updates. Every update check and download connects over TLS-encrypted HTTPS to https://macwalls-app.vercel.app.

Before any update archive is extracted or installed, its cryptographic signature is strictly verified against our public Ed25519 key, guaranteeing that only genuine, unaltered binaries can ever run on your Mac.

05. Third-Party Services & Network Requests

When searching for wallpapers or streaming video previews, the app communicates with:

  • Content Delivery Network (CDN): Standard HTTP requests to retrieve public domain and royalty-free video loops. These servers receive standard network IP logs typical of internet browsing.
  • Zero Ad Trackers: We do not integrate Google Analytics SDKs, Facebook Pixel, advertising IDs, or tracking cookies into the Macwalls desktop application.

06. User Data Control & Deletion

Because Macwalls does not maintain remote user accounts, all your application preferences, saved playlists, and favorites reside exclusively on your local Mac in macOS UserDefaults.

To completely erase all local data from your Mac at any time, delete the application from /Applications and run in Terminal:

defaults delete app.macwalls.mac
